About AVM

The Alpine Verification Meeting (AVM) is an informal, annual workshop series dedicated to formal verification. It brings together researchers including PhD students primarily from the Alpine region and surrounding countries to discuss current challenges and advances in the field.

Call for Participation

We invite researchers working in formal verification and related areas to participate in AVM'26. If you would like to give a talk, please submit a tentative title and abstract using the abstract form.

Venue

AVM'26 will be held at Hotel Korona in Eger, Hungary, a charming baroque city known for its historic castle, thermal baths, and excellent wine region. Located in the foothills of the Bükk Mountains in lieu of proper Alps, Eger offers a perfect venue for AVM'26.
